About AI Summaries

When enabled, completed scans display a Generate AI Summary button. Clicking it sends the scan's processed findings to Claude and returns a 3-4 paragraph executive narrative suitable for sharing with a prospect.

In Review mode you can read, edit, and accept the summary before it appears on any shared report link. In Auto mode it is published immediately.

Typical cost per summary: $0.01 – $0.05 depending on the number of findings and the model selected.
